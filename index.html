About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Geography at the University of Florida. I also hold an M.Sc. in Remote Sensing and GIS and a B.Sc. in Natural Resources Engineering.

My primary research integrates remote sensing, GIS, and GeoAI within Land Change Science. More recently, I have also been working on species distribution modeling and vector-borne disease research.

This website includes information about my teaching experience, publications, and CV.

Outside of my research and teaching, I enjoy playing soccer, volleyball, and pickleball.
