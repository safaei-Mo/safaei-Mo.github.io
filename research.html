Research

My research integrates remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and artificial intelligence to understand environmental and landscape change, with applications in ecology, conservation, and public health.

Remote Sensing & Land Change Science

I use satellite remote sensing, spectral mixture analysis, and long-term Earth observation data to study vegetation dynamics, land-cover change, and landscape transformation.

GeoAI & Time-Series Analysis

I apply machine learning, deep learning, change detection, and time-series modeling to geospatial data for environmental monitoring and forecasting.

Ecology & Medical Geography

I use GIS, species distribution modeling, and environmental data to investigate ecological patterns and vector-borne disease systems.
