Research

My research integrates remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and artificial intelligence to understand environmental and landscape change.

Remote Sensing & Land Change Science

I use satellite remote sensing, sub-pixel analysis, and time-series methods to study vegetation dynamics and long-term landscape change.

GeoAI & Time-Series Modeling

My work applies machine learning and deep learning methods to geospatial and Earth observation data for change detection, environmental monitoring, and forecasting.

Ecological & Public Health Applications

I also use geospatial approaches for species distribution modeling and vector-borne disease research.
