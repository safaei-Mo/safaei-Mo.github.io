Welcome

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Geography at the University of Florida. My research integrates remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and GeoAI within Land Change Science.

My work focuses on vegetation dynamics, land-cover change, time-series analysis, and ecological applications of geospatial data. I also work on species distribution modeling, vector-borne disease modeling, and UAS-based mapping.

This website highlights my research, publications, projects, teaching experience, and professional work.
